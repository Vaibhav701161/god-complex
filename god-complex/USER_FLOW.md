# GOD COMPLEX v1 — FULL USER FLOW (NO PAYMENTS)

**High-level truth first**
v1 is not the real product.
v1 is a behavioral simulator to validate:
- compliance
- friction tolerance
- enforcement credibility
- churn points

Without money, you are testing psychological enforcement, not economic enforcement.
That’s fine for v1 — if you’re honest about what you’re measuring.

## 0. ENTRY POINT (LANDING → APPLY)
**User arrives on landing page**
- They see:
    - Binary framing
    - Mechanical justice
    - Explicit exclusions
    - No dopamine promises

**What this filters**
- Casual productivity users → gone
- “Motivation seekers” → gone
- Curious but unserious → mostly gone

**Who proceeds**
- Control-oriented users
- Competitive self-improvers
- People attracted to external enforcement

**CTA: Apply for Access**
- In v1:
    - This is not a sales funnel
    - This is identity declaration

## 1. APPLICATION FLOW (V1 SIMPLIFIED)
**Step 1: Account creation**
- Email + password
- Email verification (mandatory)
- No OAuth in v1 — deliberate friction.
*Why: Signals seriousness, Reduces throwaway users*

**Step 2: Application form (critical)**
- This is NOT a formality.
- You collect:
    - Primary objective (free text)
    - Time availability (hours/day)
    - Prior consistency attempts (checkboxes)
    - Agreement to non-negotiable rules (explicit consent)
*Important: No psychometric analysis yet. No AI judgment yet. You are logging intent vs later behavior. This data becomes future fraud detection input.*

**Step 3: Manual or auto-approval (v1 choice)**
- For v1: Auto-approve after rules acceptance
- But log:
    - timestamp
    - IP
    - agreement hash
*This matters later when users dispute enforcement.*

## 2. ONBOARDING: SYSTEM INDOCTRINATION (NON-OPTIONAL)
**Step 4: Rules acknowledgment screen**
Before anything else, user must:
- Scroll through rules
- Check acknowledgment boxes
- Click “Initialize Protocol”
*No skip. No tutorial fluff. If they bounce here → GOOD. They would have churned later anyway.*

**Step 5: Group assignment (v1 simplified)**
- In v1: Default to solo mode
- Groups optional / mocked
*Why: Groups add coordination complexity. Enforcement logic must work solo first. Group logic can exist in backend but UI can hide it.*

## 3. DAILY OPERATIONAL LOOP (CORE OF V1)
This is the heart of God Complex.

**A. DAY START: GOAL DECLARATION (TIME-LOCKED)**
- **Trigger**: Daily window opens (e.g., 5:00–9:00 AM local time)
- **User must**: Declare 1–3 goals max
- Each goal must include:
    - measurable condition
    - discomfort flag (yes/no)
    - estimated duration
- **Hard rules**:
    - No edits after submission
    - Missed declaration = automatic failure day
*Why: Prevents retroactive rationalization. Forces planning under constraint.*

**B. EXECUTION PHASE (PASSIVE, NOT TRACKED)**
- During the day: God Complex does nothing
- No reminders
- No nudges
- No motivational content
*This is intentional. You are testing: internal execution under external accountability, not habit formation UX.*

**C. DAY END: RESOLUTION WINDOW**
- **Trigger**: Fixed nightly window (e.g., 9:00–11:00 PM)
- **User must**: For each goal, Mark Completed or Failed
- If Failed → select excuse category
- **Excuse categories are**:
    - finite
    - predefined
    - emotionally cold
*No free text justification in v1 (important).*

**D. EXCUSE TAX SYSTEM (V1 LOGIC)**
- When user selects an excuse: System increments excuse counters
- Repeated excuses increase penalty weight
- Example:
    - Same excuse 2× in 7 days → next failure auto-zero day
    - 3× → streak reset
*Critical: Even without money, penalties must hurt structurally: score damage, streak destruction, visible failure markers.*

## 4. SCORING & FEEDBACK (WHERE V1 IS WEAK)
**Daily score computation**
- Binary completion
- Consistency-weighted
- Failure compounds negatively

**User sees**:
- Score graph
- Streak status
- Excuse frequency

**What you do NOT show in v1**:
- Praise
- Emotional feedback
- Encouragement
*Cold data only.*

**Reality check**
Without money:
- Some users will brute-force failures
- Some will lie (mark complete falsely)
- You cannot stop this in v1.
*What you can do: Detect patterns, Measure dishonesty tolerance, Identify future exploit vectors.*

## 5. WEEKLY & MONTHLY AGGREGATION
**Weekly summary**
User sees:
- % compliance
- Most used excuse
- Worst day of week
- Trend direction
*No coaching. No advice.*

**Monthly resolution (no payouts in v1)**
- Since no payment: “Winner / Loser” is symbolic
- Status badge only
- Archive of month is immutable
*This is where v1 feels fake — and that’s okay. You are validating: whether people care even without money, who sticks around regardless.*

## 6. FAILURE STATES (IMPORTANT)
**Automatic failures**
- Missed declaration
- Missed resolution
- Too many repeated excuses
*These must feel: immediate, non-negotiable, irreversible. If you allow reversals in v1, you destroy future credibility.*

## 7. EXIT & CHURN
**Users leave in 3 ways**:
- Silent churn (stop logging)
- Explicit quit
- Rage quit after enforcement

*All three are success signals, not failures.*
*Why: You are not building a mass app. You are building a filtering machine.*
